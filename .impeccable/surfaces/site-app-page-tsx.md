# Landing page brief

Scope: closetalk.dev home page plus /privacy. Visitor mode: Persuade. Code-led build (no image generation).

Audience: anyone on a Mac who prompts AI agents; developers first, never only developers. Job: understand Closetalk in one viewport and download it. Action: Download for Mac (releases/latest/download/Closetalk.dmg). Proof on hand: open source (MIT) and measured timings. No quotes for now (the user chose to launch without them); never invent quotes, counts or benchmarks.

Constraints: no third-party requests at runtime (fonts self-hosted, no analytics). Demo clip is a stub until Gautham records it. Anti-goals: generic SaaS, too precious, developers only, salesy.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The Riso mic printed as a labeled product diagram: every leader line points at one reason to download. Refuses the split hero with a feature-card row.

OWN-WORLD: Uncoated paper ground with fiber grain. Two inks only, fluorescent pink #ed269d and blue #1c81c3, overprinting to purple #4a147d for text. Bricolage Grotesque for display and body. Thin blue leader lines with round pins, halftone mic art printed with multiply, wordmark printed twice off-register.

STORY: The visitor reads the headline, follows the four callouts around the mic (runs on your Mac, hold your shortcut, your exact words, free with no account), downloads from the mic's base, then scrolls to the free-because-local argument, the demo and the questions.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Headline across the top. The mic large and centered, four callouts at its sides joined by leader lines to pins on the mic. Download for Mac plus requirements printed on the mic's base, centered under it.

FORM: Mic anatomy, position 5 of 7, seed 0d395c1e.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
